About RecycLens

Making plastic waste sorting smarter and more sustainable

Our Mission

RecycLens is an AI-powered plastic waste classification system designed to streamline recycling processes and promote sustainable waste management. By leveraging machine learning and computer vision, we help identify different plastic types instantly, enabling more effective sorting and recycling.

How It Works

1

Upload Image

Take a photo of plastic waste or upload an existing image of the material you want to classify.

2

AI Analysis

Our machine learning model analyzes the image and identifies the plastic type based on visual characteristics.

3

Get Results

Receive instant classification results including recyclability information and environmental impact data.

4

Take Action

Use the recycling guidance to properly sort your plastic waste for maximum environmental benefit.

Technology Stack

RecycLens is built with modern technologies:

  • Backend: Python Flask - Lightweight web framework
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript - Responsive user interface
  • ML Framework: TensorFlow/Keras - Deep learning model training and inference
  • Image Processing: Pillow, NumPy - Image manipulation and preprocessing
  • Data: TrashNet, Kaggle datasets - Open-source plastic waste datasets

Environmental Impact

Our goal is to contribute to a more sustainable future. By improving plastic waste sorting efficiency:

  • Reduce contamination: Automated sorting minimizes contamination in recycling streams, allowing more plastic to be successfully recycled.
  • Lower costs: Efficient sorting reduces labor costs and improves facility economics, making recycling more viable.
  • Higher quality materials: Better sorted plastics produce higher quality recycled materials that can be reused in more applications.
  • Support circular economy: Increased recycling rates help establish circular economy models where plastics are reused rather than discarded.
  • Ocean protection: Proper waste management prevents plastic from entering oceans and harming marine ecosystems.

Getting Started with Model Integration

RecycLens is currently running with a placeholder model for demonstration. To integrate your trained TensorFlow/Keras model:

  1. Train your model on the TrashNet or Kaggle plastic datasets
  2. Save your model: model.save('models/plastic_classifier.h5')
  3. Update models/classifier.py to load and use your model
  4. Test classification accuracy on validation images
  5. Deploy to production with your trained model
